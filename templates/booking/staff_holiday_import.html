Example rows only. Use your company holiday schedule.

  • Name the sheet Holidays and use the headers Date and Occasion.
  • Use a Gregorian/AD Excel date cell or text in YYYY-MM-DD format.
  • Keep the occasion name within 160 characters.
  • Enter one row per date, including each day of a multi-day holiday.
  • Avoid duplicate dates, merged cells and formulas.
