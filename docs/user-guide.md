# Employee guide

1. Open the internal WDN Meeting Rooms address and enter your `wdn.com.np` email. Open the one-time link in your company mailbox and confirm sign-in. Keep the same browser and internal address throughout sign-in.
2. Open **Rooms** to compare seating capacity, facilities, and room instructions. Use **Calendar** to see free and occupied time. The day view has 15-minute rows; selecting a free row starts a booking. The week view gives an overview.
3. On **New booking**, choose a room, working date, start/end time, meeting type, title, attendee email addresses, and any Front Desk notes. For external meetings, enter the guest company and guest count. Select daily, weekly, or monthly recurrence if needed. Only occurrences within the next two weeks are reserved; book later months when they enter that window.
4. Check the confirmation page and email. If a room becomes unavailable before your booking saves, choose a new room or time. Bookings require a 15-minute gap, run during configured working hours, and cannot overlap room closures or another meeting.
5. Open **My bookings** to edit or cancel your own reservation. Each occurrence in a repeating series is managed separately. You may extend a meeting by editing its end time when the room is free.
6. One hour before the meeting, the organizer receives a reminder with a secure check-in link. Open it **after the meeting starts** and check in within 15 minutes. If you do not check in, the booking is automatically marked no-show and the room is released. Front Desk can help with a manual check-in during the same window.

Other employees see room occupancy but cannot open your meeting details, notes, or attendee list. Contact Front Desk for booking help and company IT for technical problems.
